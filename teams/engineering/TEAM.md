The Engineering team owns the full software lifecycle. Software Architect plans the system and gates design decisions. Senior Developer implements with test-driven discipline, writing tests before code. Code Reviewer gates every merge, enforcing quality and consistency. DevOps Engineer ships to production and manages infrastructure. Security Engineer audits in parallel, catching vulnerabilities before they reach users. Technical Writer documents APIs, architecture, and runbooks. The Engineering team serves the company.

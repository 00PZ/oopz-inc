The Production team turns Researcher dossiers into approved drafts. Writer produces platform-native content (rethinking, not reformatting), Editor gates every draft for voice fidelity, platform-nativeness, hook quality, and compliance. No draft reaches Scheduler without Editor approval. The Production team serves the company.

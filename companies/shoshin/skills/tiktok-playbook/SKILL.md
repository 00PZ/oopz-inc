## Format Rules

- Video: 15-60s optimal (up to 10 min allowed); vertical 9:16 required
- Captions: 2200 char limit; first 150 chars visible before "more"
- Hashtags: 3-5 relevant; avoid hashtag stuffing
- Audio: trending sounds boost reach significantly

## Cadence

- Target: 1-2 posts/day; max 2/day
- Best times: 07:00-09:00 and 19:00-21:00 Europe/Amsterdam

## Hook Discipline

See `[[hooks-library]]`. On TikTok: first 3 seconds are everything. Pattern-interrupt and personal-stake hooks dominate. Verbal hook must match visual hook.

## Voice Variant

See `[[brand-voice-system]]`. TikTok-specific: high-energy verbal, conversational, fast-paced. Speak TO the camera. Use "you" frequently. Avoid reading from a script.

## Content-Type Matrix

From `[[content-types]]`: reel/short, behind-the-scenes, community question, case-study (story format), hook-only bait.

## Compliance Overlay

See `[[compliance-rules]]`. On TikTok: verbal disclosure ("this is not financial advice") + caption disclosure. Paid partnerships: TikTok's built-in branded content toggle.

## Native Output Example

```
Caption: I spent 3 months researching DePIN telecom so you don't have to. Here's what I found about World Mobile 👇 #DePIN #WorldMobile #crypto #telecom

[Video: 45s explainer with on-screen text highlights]
Not financial advice. DYOR.
```

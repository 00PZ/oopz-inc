## Format Rules

- Feed carousel: 1080x1350 (portrait) up to 10 slides; first slide is the hook
- Reels: 9:16 vertical, 15-90s optimal
- Stories: 1080x1920, 15s per frame
- Caption: 2200 char limit; first 125 chars visible before "more"

## Cadence

- Target: 1 feed post + 3-5 stories/day
- Best times: 11:00-13:00 and 19:00-21:00 Europe/Amsterdam

## Hook Discipline

See `[[hooks-library]]`. On Instagram: visual-first, the first slide IS the hook. Curiosity-gap and status-play hooks work well. Caption hook in first 125 chars.

## Voice Variant

See `[[brand-voice-system]]`. Instagram-specific: aspirational, visual-first, slightly more polished than X. Still direct and non-corporate. Emojis used sparingly for emphasis.

## Content-Type Matrix

From `[[content-types]]`: carousel, reel/short, behind-the-scenes, listicle (carousel format), data visualization (infographic carousel).

## Compliance Overlay

See `[[compliance-rules]]`. On Instagram: paid partnership tag + caption disclosure. For crypto/finance: "Not financial advice. DYOR." in caption. Sticker overlay for sponsored stories.

## Native Output Example

```
Slide 1: "The telecom company you've never heard of, but should know about"
Slide 2: "World Mobile is building community-owned cell towers"
...
Caption: DePIN telecom explained in 8 slides. Not financial advice. DYOR. #DePIN #WorldMobile
```

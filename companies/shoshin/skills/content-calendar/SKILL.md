# Content Calendar

All times and schedules are in **Europe/Amsterdam** timezone.

## Cadence Caps (Europe/Amsterdam)

| Platform  | Target/day            | Max/day               |
|-----------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| X         | 3                     | 5                     |
| TikTok    | 1                     | 2                     |
| Instagram | 1 feed + 3 stories   | 1 feed + 5 stories   |
| Threads   | 2                     | 4                     |

### Why Caps Matter

Overposting dilutes engagement per post and trains the algorithm to deprioritize your content. Hitting max/day should be rare and intentional (e.g., breaking news, product launch).

## Weekly Rhythm

- **Monday**: Original long-form thread (X) + carousel (Instagram)
- **Tuesday**: Reply/engagement day, respond to comments, quote-tweet signals
- **Wednesday**: Short burst, hot-take (Threads) + TikTok
- **Thursday**: Repurpose peak, take Monday's best-performing angle to other platforms
- **Friday**: Recap/list format, weekly summary thread (X) + Instagram story recap
- **Saturday-Sunday**: Light, reactive, community questions, polls, low-effort engagement

### Rhythm Notes

- Thursday repurposing uses the `[[repurpose-engine]]` workflow. Only repurpose content that performed above baseline on Monday.
- Weekend content should require minimal production effort. Pre-schedule Friday evening where possible.
- Adjust rhythm during product launches or major announcements (Strategist overrides).

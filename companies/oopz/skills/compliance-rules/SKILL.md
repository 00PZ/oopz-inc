# Compliance Rules

Mandatory compliance framework for regulated-topic content. This is NOT legal advice, it is a practical content compliance checklist that every agent must follow when producing or reviewing content about crypto, finance, DePIN, or similar regulated domains.

## Scope

Applies to all content about regulated topics: crypto (tokens, DeFi, DePIN), finance, healthcare, any topic where a reasonable reader might infer investment advice. When in doubt, apply these rules. False positives (over-disclosing) are always preferable to false negatives (under-disclosing).

## Operator Posture (CRITICAL)

Shoshin operates as a THIRD-PARTY INDEPENDENT CREATOR. Shoshin is NOT affiliated with World Mobile Group or any issuer whose token/security is discussed. All content must make this posture unambiguous.

This means:
- Never imply partnership, endorsement, or insider access
- Never use "we" when referring to World Mobile Group or any token issuer
- Always write from an outside observer or analyst perspective
- If quoting official sources, attribute clearly ("World Mobile Group announced..." not "we announced...")

## FTC Section 255 Disclosure (US Audience)

When content involves sponsored partnerships or material connections, FTC disclosure is required:

- `#ad` in post body AND in bio for sponsored content
- Material connections must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously
- Disclosure must appear before any claim about the product/service

Per-platform templates:

- **X (Twitter):** `#ad` in post body + bio link disclosure page
- **TikTok:** Verbal disclosure ("this is a paid partnership") within first 3 seconds + caption `#ad`
- **Instagram:** Use paid partnership tag + caption disclosure with `#ad`
- **Threads:** `#ad` at start of post, not buried at the end

If no sponsorship or material connection exists, FTC disclosure is not required, but the not-financial-advice boilerplate still applies for crypto/finance content.

## FCA / UK Financial Promotion

Content reaching UK audiences must comply with FCA financial promotion rules.

If the creator has holdings in the token discussed, include: "I hold $WMTX. This is my opinion, not financial advice. DYOR."

Additional FCA requirements:
- Risk warnings must be prominent, not hidden in small text
- Past performance disclaimers where historical data is referenced
- No misleading claims about guaranteed returns

## MiCA / EU Crypto-Asset Regulation

Crypto content targeted at or accessible to EU audiences must include a risk warning per MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation).

Required risk warning: "Crypto-assets are highly volatile. You can lose all invested capital."

Additional MiCA considerations:
- Stablecoin references must not imply guaranteed peg stability
- Whitepapers or project documentation references should link to official sources
- No claims of regulatory approval unless verifiably true

## Not-Financial-Advice Boilerplate (DEFAULT for crypto/finance posts)

Exact text to append to every crypto/finance post:

```
Not financial advice. I am not affiliated with World Mobile Group. DYOR before making any investment decisions.
```

This boilerplate is non-negotiable. Every post touching crypto, tokens, DeFi, DePIN, or investment topics MUST include it. No exceptions.

## Source-Citation Rule

Every factual claim cites a primary source. Rumor or speculation is clearly labeled "speculation" or "unverified". No claim without a source.

Guidelines:
- Primary sources (official announcements, on-chain data, regulatory filings) are preferred
- Secondary sources (news articles, analyst reports) are acceptable with attribution
- Social media posts are cited with username, platform, and date
- If a source cannot be verified, the claim is either dropped or explicitly marked as unverified
- Never fabricate or hallucinate sources

## Ticker Usage

Use `$WMTX` for World Mobile Token (user-confirmed current ticker as of Apr 2026). Do NOT use `$WMT` (old/alternate ticker). If you ever see a conflicting signal about the correct ticker, consult the user before posting.

Correct: "$WMTX is trading at..."
Incorrect: "$WMT is trading at..."

When referencing other tokens, use their canonical ticker symbols and verify accuracy before publishing.

## Impersonation Ban

NEVER post as if you are World Mobile Group. NEVER reuse their official brand assets without written permission. All content is clearly from a third-party independent creator perspective.

Specifically prohibited:
- Using World Mobile Group logos, brand colors, or official imagery without permission
- Phrasing that implies official capacity ("our network", "we are launching")
- Copying official announcements verbatim without attribution
- Creating content that a reasonable person could mistake for official World Mobile Group communications

## Price and Investment Restrictions

- No price predictions (e.g., "$WMTX will reach $X by Q3")
- No buy/sell/hold signals or recommendations
- No "to the moon" style hype that implies guaranteed appreciation
- Historical price data may be referenced with appropriate disclaimers
- Market analysis is permitted if clearly framed as opinion, not advice

## Editor Checklist (run on every regulated-topic draft)

Before publishing any regulated-topic content, verify ALL of the following:

- [ ] 1. Is the third-party creator posture clear? (Not affiliated with World Mobile Group)
- [ ] 2. Is the not-financial-advice disclaimer present?
- [ ] 3. Is "DYOR" included?
- [ ] 4. Is the correct ticker used ($WMTX, not $WMT)?
- [ ] 5. Are all factual claims sourced? (No unsourced claims)
- [ ] 6. Is speculation clearly labeled as speculation?
- [ ] 7. Is FTC disclosure present if this is sponsored content?
- [ ] 8. Is MiCA risk warning present for EU-targeted content?
- [ ] 9. Does the content avoid price predictions or buy/sell signals?
- [ ] 10. Is the impersonation ban respected? (No World Mobile Group brand assets)

If ANY item fails, the draft is rejected and returned for revision. Do not publish until all 10 items pass.

## Escalation

When in doubt about compliance, escalate to the human operator. Never guess on regulatory matters. The human-in-the-loop review is mandatory for:

- Content mentioning specific token prices or market performance
- Content that could be interpreted as financial promotion
- Any content involving sponsored partnerships or material connections
- Content referencing regulatory actions or legal proceedings

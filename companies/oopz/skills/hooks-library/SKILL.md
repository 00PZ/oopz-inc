# Hooks Library

A living catalog of hook patterns for short-form content. Each hook includes a template, when to use it, platform fit, and a concrete example. Organized by psychological trigger category.

## Contrarian Hooks

1. **"Nobody talks about [X]"**
   - Template: `Nobody talks about [surprising aspect of topic]`
   - When to use: Challenging a mainstream narrative or surfacing an overlooked angle.
   - Platform fit: X, Threads
   - Example: "nobody talks about how DePIN is solving the last-mile problem"

2. **"Hot take: [controversial claim]"**
   - Template: `Hot take: [bold, defensible opinion]`
   - When to use: Sparking debate or staking a position.
   - Platform fit: Threads, X
   - Example: "hot take: the best telecom play isn't a telco"

3. **"[Popular belief] is wrong. Here's why:"**
   - Template: `[Widely accepted idea] is wrong. Here's why:`
   - When to use: Debunking a common assumption with evidence.
   - Platform fit: X threads
   - Example: "crypto infrastructure is boring is wrong. here's why:"

4. **"Everyone is focused on [X]. They're missing [Y]."**
   - Template: `Everyone is focused on [obvious thing]. They're missing [hidden thing].`
   - When to use: Redirecting attention from the obvious to the overlooked.
   - Platform fit: X, TikTok
   - Example: "everyone is focused on the token. they're missing the infrastructure."

5. **"The [industry] doesn't want you to know this:"**
   - Template: `The [industry/establishment] doesn't want you to know this:`
   - When to use: Insider framing, revealing hidden dynamics.
   - Platform fit: TikTok, X
   - Example: "the telecom industry doesn't want you to know this:"

## Curiosity-Gap Hooks

6. **"I spent [time] researching [X] so you don't have to. Here's what I found:"**
   - Template: `I spent [specific timeframe] researching [topic] so you don't have to. Here's what I found:`
   - When to use: Research summary, positioning as the person who did the work.
   - Platform fit: TikTok, Instagram
   - Example: "I spent 3 months researching DePIN telecom so you don't have to."

7. **"The [number] things nobody tells you about [X]:"**
   - Template: `The [number] things nobody tells you about [topic]:`
   - When to use: List format with an insider knowledge angle.
   - Platform fit: Instagram carousel, X thread
   - Example: "the 5 things nobody tells you about community-owned networks"

8. **"What happens when [scenario]? I tested it."**
   - Template: `What happens when [specific action/scenario]? I tested it.`
   - When to use: Experiment framing, personal proof.
   - Platform fit: TikTok, X
   - Example: "what happens when you run an Earth Node for 30 days? I tested it."

9. **"This [thing] changed how I think about [topic]:"**
   - Template: `This [specific thing] changed how I think about [broad topic]:`
   - When to use: Perspective shift, intellectual curiosity trigger.
   - Platform fit: Threads, X
   - Example: "this whitepaper changed how I think about mobile connectivity"

## Numeric Hooks

10. **"[Number] reasons [claim]:"**
    - Template: `[Number] reasons [bold claim]:`
    - When to use: Listicle format, clear value proposition.
    - Platform fit: all
    - Example: "3 reasons DePIN telecom is different from every other crypto project"

11. **"[Number]% of [audience] don't know [fact]:"**
    - Template: `[Number]% of [specific audience] don't know [surprising fact]:`
    - When to use: Stat-based hook, knowledge gap framing.
    - Platform fit: X, Instagram
    - Example: "90% of crypto investors don't know how Earth Nodes work"

12. **"In [timeframe], [X] went from [A] to [B]:"**
    - Template: `In [specific timeframe], [subject] went from [starting state] to [end state]:`
    - When to use: Growth story, transformation narrative.
    - Platform fit: X, TikTok
    - Example: "in 18 months, World Mobile went from whitepaper to live network"

13. **"[Number] [things] that will [outcome]:"**
    - Template: `[Number] [category items] that will [future outcome]:`
    - When to use: Prediction list, forward-looking authority.
    - Platform fit: X thread, Instagram
    - Example: "5 DePIN projects that will matter in 2025"

## Personal-Stake Hooks

14. **"I [action] and here's what happened:"**
    - Template: `I [specific personal action] and here's what happened:`
    - When to use: Personal story, first-person experience.
    - Platform fit: TikTok, Threads
    - Example: "I ran an Earth Node for 6 months and here's what happened:"

15. **"Why I changed my mind about [X]:"**
    - Template: `Why I changed my mind about [topic/opinion]:`
    - When to use: Opinion shift, intellectual honesty signal.
    - Platform fit: X, Threads
    - Example: "why I changed my mind about DePIN infrastructure"

16. **"My honest take on [X] after [time/experience]:"**
    - Template: `My honest take on [subject] after [specific time or experience]:`
    - When to use: Review format, earned authority.
    - Platform fit: all
    - Example: "my honest take on World Mobile after 6 months of research"

## Question Hooks

17. **"What if [scenario]?"**
    - Template: `What if [thought-provoking hypothetical]?`
    - When to use: Thought experiment, imagination trigger.
    - Platform fit: Threads, X
    - Example: "what if your phone bill funded the network it runs on?"

18. **"Have you ever wondered why [X]?"**
    - Template: `Have you ever wondered why [commonly accepted but unexplained thing]?`
    - When to use: Curiosity trigger, shared experience framing.
    - Platform fit: TikTok, Instagram
    - Example: "have you ever wondered why mobile data is so expensive in Africa?"

## Pattern-Interrupt Hooks

19. **"[Unexpected statement]. Let me explain."**
    - Template: `[Shocking or counterintuitive claim]. Let me explain.`
    - When to use: Shocking claim that demands justification.
    - Platform fit: TikTok, X
    - Example: "your phone is a liability. let me explain."

20. **"Stop [common behavior]. Do [alternative] instead."**
    - Template: `Stop [what everyone does]. Do [better alternative] instead.`
    - When to use: Behavior change prompt, authority positioning.
    - Platform fit: TikTok, Instagram
    - Example: "stop buying tokens. buy infrastructure instead."

> **Iterate weekly**: Analyst proposes new hooks from top performers. Human approves and appends. This library compounds over time.
